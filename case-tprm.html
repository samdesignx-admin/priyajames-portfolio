Case study 02 · Third-party risk

Making manual TPRM a durable operating model.

An organization wanted to move manual cybersecurity IRM and third-party risk activities into ServiceNow. A heavily customized legacy implementation had left teams reliant on workarounds and reduced confidence in the tool.

ServiceNow IRMTPRMPolicy & complianceProduct security

The opportunity

Bring the process closer to the platform.

The work focused on an out-of-the-box approach that could scale with the organization. It meant creating automation where the process was manual, while still giving teams the flexibility to manage policy and compliance, risk, vendors, and product security assessments in one coherent environment.

Priya’s role

Translate pain points into a practical, adopted path forward.

01

Listen

Conducted stakeholder sessions to understand the current IRM and TPRM environment, target state, and gaps left by the prior implementation.

02

Reframe

Applied industry practices to enhance policy and control management, risk assessment methodology, and third-party risk workflows.

03

Connect

Enabled policy and compliance, advanced risk, product-security assessments, and third-party risk flows integrated with procurement, then supported change management and training.

What changed

A future-ready IRM foundation with room to grow.

One source of truth

Standardized IRM data and processes that could be used across the organization.

Scalable delivery

Less unnecessary customization and a more maintainable way to run IRM and TPRM.

Clearer decisions

Greater visibility into vendors, risk, compliance posture, and the next implementation opportunities.
