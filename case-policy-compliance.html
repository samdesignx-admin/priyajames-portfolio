Case study 01 · GRC / IRM

Making policy, compliance, audit, and risk work as one.

A cybersecurity organization needed to mature its IRM program. Disparate data and manual processes were making it difficult to manage compliance, assess risk, and give teams a shared view of control.

ServiceNow IRMPolicy & complianceAuditAdvanced risk assessment

The opportunity

From fragmented activity to a system people could trust.

The work began with a simple but meaningful challenge: create an IRM foundation that could support both the cybersecurity team and the people responsible for controls across the organization. The goal was not merely to configure modules; it was to connect requirements, assets, processes, evidence, and decisions in a way that made the program easier to operate and easier to understand.

Priya’s role

Design the operating model, then make it usable.

01

Discover

Facilitated working sessions to identify, assess, and validate critical internal and external compliance requirements.

02

Design

Developed a plan for third-party risk, policy and compliance, audit, and advanced risk assessment, supported by ServiceNow demonstrations and a common data model.

03

Enable

Mapped security requirements to relevant IT assets, led end-user testing, and created training materials and technical documentation for knowledge transfer.

What changed

More than a new platform: a clearer way to run risk.

Foundation

A robust framework for third-party risk, regulatory requirements, cybersecurity policies, and controls.

Visibility

Tailored workflows, reports, and dashboards supporting better operational insight and data accuracy.

Adoption

30+ policies and standards, 80+ automated requirements, and adoption across hundreds of users.
