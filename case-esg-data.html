Case study 07 · ESG data

Making emissions data operational.

A multi-entity organization needed to translate Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse-gas reporting needs into a ServiceNow ESG Management data model that teams could genuinely operate.

ServiceNow ESGEmissions dataData governanceDiscovery

The opportunity

Move from a requirements workbook to a governed, usable data model.

Buildings, vehicles, utility data, emission factors, and distinct reporting calculations all needed to make sense together. The challenge was to establish a shared language between reporting requirements and platform configuration, while creating a foundation two entities could use consistently.

Priya’s role

Translate reporting requirements into the building blocks of an ESG program.

01

Discover

Led statement-of-work development and discovery, clarifying sources, data needs, ownership, and the desired reporting model.

02

Model

Converted field-level requirements into ServiceNow-native concepts: entities, activity-data templates, and governed emission-factor reference data.

03

Differentiate

Created a baseline platform view that separated out-of-the-box capability from the configuration choices specific to the organization.

What changed

A clearer foundation for ESG reporting and delivery.

Shared model

A data-model view that both stakeholders and delivery teams could build from.

Phased plan

A practical route through Scope 1 sources and location- and market-based Scope 2 calculations.

Governed data

Reference data and templates designed for consistency as reporting needs evolve.
