Case study 04 · Cloud governance

A cloud-service adoption process built for speed and control.

A Fortune 100 financial institution needed to govern a growing and continuously changing cloud-service landscape. Existing approval and control processes were difficult to follow, and there was no organized way to reassess services once they changed.

Cloud governanceThird-party riskServiceNowPerformance analytics

The opportunity

Make cloud adoption more transparent without slowing the business down.

The program needed to create a clear service-adoption journey, establish meaningful control expectations, and give decision-makers a way to see where services were in the process. This was a governance challenge as much as a platform one: the right data, workflow, ownership, and reporting had to work together.

Priya’s role

Connect cloud services to the controls that make adoption responsible.

01

Align

Worked across process and technical stakeholders with third-party risk and cloud-service expertise, supported by a unified platform architecture.

02

Improve

Applied GRC, vendor-risk, and performance-analytics experience to identify best practices and practical process improvements.

03

Operationalize

Partnered with platform owners to address strategic, technical, and operational governance needs within the existing ServiceNow estate.

What changed

A more visible, repeatable path to governed cloud adoption.

Harmonized process

Workflow-driven assessments standardized the cloud-service adoption process and clarified control expectations.

Transparent catalog

A cloud-service catalog provided visibility into approval status, versioning, service status, and compensating controls.

Strategic roadmap

A platform roadmap helped prioritize the next generation of integration and cloud-governance capability.
