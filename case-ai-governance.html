Case study 10 · AI governance

Bringing AI risk into the enterprise GRC conversation.

Organizations adopting AI needed governance frameworks that could keep up with the technology while remaining connected to the regulatory, ethical, and operational standards already guiding the rest of the business.

AI governanceServiceNow GRCRisk & controlsEnterprise integration

The opportunity

Do not build a parallel process for an enterprise-wide risk.

The work focused on making AI governance a natural part of enterprise risk management, rather than an isolated initiative. The task was to develop risk and control frameworks that were responsive to an evolving technology while also usable within the governance structures organizations already relied on.

Priya’s role

Turn an emerging concern into a scalable governance capability.

01

Contextualize

Identified where AI use cases created new questions for existing risk, control, compliance, and ethical decision-making processes.

02

Design

Developed scalable AI risk and control frameworks within ServiceNow GRC/IRM environments.

03

Integrate

Connected emerging-technology governance directly to broader enterprise GRC programs rather than standing up a separate process.

What changed

A more mature way to govern innovation as it happens.

Alignment

AI risk considered through the same regulatory and ethical lens as other enterprise risks.

Scalability

Risk and control frameworks designed to evolve as AI adoption develops.

Integration

Emerging-technology governance connected to the systems and teams already managing risk.
