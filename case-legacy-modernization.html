Case study 03 · Platform modernization

Replacing GRC complexity with an IRM future.

A regulated organization needed to move its GRC processes from multiple legacy tools and an existing bespoke build to a refreshed, out-of-the-box ServiceNow IRM environment — on an accelerated timeline.

ServiceNow IRMLegacy modernizationIntegrationsChange management

The opportunity

Modernize the platform without leaving people behind.

Inconsistent processes, regulatory commitments, and resistance to change made this much more than a technology replacement. The program needed a partner who could lead process design, technical execution, program governance, and adoption as a single integrated effort.

Priya’s role

Bring business process, technology, and change together.

01

Design

Created leading-practice process recommendations across IRM foundations, policy, compliance, issue management, monitoring, third-party risk, assessments, regulatory change, and metrics.

02

Integrate

Led integrations with enterprise systems supporting financial, regulatory-change, and procurement workflows.

03

Embed

Managed a multi-year program, developed a sustainable user-access strategy, and executed change management and training for rollout.

What changed

A GRC environment designed to create accountability, not friction.

Modern foundation

A new GRC platform supporting greater first-line ownership of risk and controls.

Better insight

More timely executive and board reporting on risk profile and issue management.

Lasting adoption

High adoption rates supported by intentional organizational change and business readiness.
