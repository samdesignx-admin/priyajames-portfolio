Case study 08 · Practice build

Building a repeatable IRM practice around what clients actually need.

An emerging ServiceNow IRM offering needed a clear market position, reusable accelerators, and a delivery methodology that could bring consistency to both the sales conversation and the work itself.

Practice strategyIRM acceleratorsPresalesDelivery methodology

The opportunity

Give a strong offering a point of view and a way to repeat it.

The challenge was to create a practical alternative to generic, one-size-fits-all GRC delivery. Priya focused on the tools, language, and frameworks that help a team explain its value clearly, deliver with confidence, and leave clients with an operating model that lasts.

Priya’s role

Create the intellectual and operational assets behind consistent delivery.

01

Accelerate

Built reusable risk and control libraries and entity-governance frameworks that give projects a more valuable starting point.

02

Articulate

Developed executive-ready positioning around an Assess / Advise / Implement / Operate delivery framework.

03

Extend

Created dedicated security-operations presales material and a growing collection of module-level guidance for the wider team.

What changed

A clearer offer, a more repeatable method, and stronger delivery confidence.

Methodology

A repeatable, practical GRC delivery approach for the middle market.

Assets

Reusable collateral and accelerators that create a more consistent start to every engagement.

Momentum

A stronger presales story grounded in how the work is actually delivered.
