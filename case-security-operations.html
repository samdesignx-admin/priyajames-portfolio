Case study 05 · Security operations

One security operations platform. A clearer response.

A major financial institution had a disconnected global ecosystem of people and platforms. Security incidents were slower to resolve, accountability was hard to trace, and manual measures made proactive risk management difficult.

Security operationsIncident responsePlatform strategyRisk visualization

The opportunity

Move from disconnected activity to a shared, mature security operating model.

The organization needed more than incident ticketing. It needed an integrated platform strategy that could bring people, workflows, data, automation, and executive visibility together — while making it easier to onboard new teams and build new capabilities over time.

Priya’s role

Create the operational backbone for faster, more informed response.

01

Unify

Worked with cross-functional specialists and client stakeholders to connect security-operations processes across different workstreams.

02

Illuminate

Used platform expertise to create seamless reporting at every level, from actionable team dashboards to high-visibility stakeholder views.

03

Advance

Partnered with platform owners to resolve strategic, technical, and operational governance pain points and create a forward-looking maturity path.

What changed

A security operation designed to see, decide, and respond.

Visibility

Value-based prioritization, clearer performance signals, and reporting that connected impact to response.

Context

Enhanced incident enrichment and automated data gathering to improve detection quality and reduce time to insight.

Response

Automated remediation actions, real-time risk visualization, and dynamic workflows that strengthened the response cycle.
