Case study 09 · Applied AI

GRC expertise, a click away.

Delivery teams needed a faster way to reason through dense platform mechanics, attestation workflows, control objectives, and resilience positioning — without waiting for a single subject-matter expert’s calendar.

Applied AIInternal toolingServiceNow GRCKnowledge enablement

The opportunity

Make the depth of the practice easier to access in the moment it is needed.

The project was an exploration of a simple idea: deep delivery knowledge should be available to the people doing the work, not trapped in documents or dependent on a few experts. Priya designed a subject-matter agent that turns complex reference material into a practical team resource.

Priya’s role

Design a useful tool around the questions teams ask every day.

01

Define

Identified high-value knowledge domains including security incident response, IRM attestation workflows, control mapping, and operational resilience.

02

Build

Designed and developed a custom React-based GRC subject-matter agent with platform-level context at its core.

03

Apply

Used the build as a hands-on model for how AI can strengthen the day-to-day operations of a GRC delivery practice.

What changed

A more immediate path from question to practical expertise.

Access

Platform-level GRC knowledge available when delivery teams need it.

Consistency

A reference point that supports clearer, more aligned technical reasoning.

Learning

A working internal example of AI’s role in the operations of a GRC practice.
